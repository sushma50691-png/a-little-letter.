01 · a letter from me

If my heart could
write a letter

These are the things I want you to understand, gently and honestly.

Hey, my love. If you're reading this, I hope you're comfortable and taking a little moment for yourself. I made this page because there are feelings I sometimes struggle to explain when we're talking. Sometimes I have so much on my mind that the important things get lost in the middle of a conversation.

First, I want you to know that you matter to me. You matter in the ordinary parts of my day, in the little things that remind me of you, and in the moments when I wish I could tell you something immediately because you're the person I want to share it with.

I don't need our relationship to look perfect from the outside. I don't need every day to be magical or every conversation to be beautiful. I want something real. Something where we can have difficult days and still treat each other with care. Something where we can admit when we're wrong and try to do better without making each other feel small for having feelings.

And because what we have matters to me, I want to be honest about what has been sitting on my heart. Sometimes I feel hurt when something we talked about or promised doesn't happen, especially when it happens more than once. Sometimes I need reassurance and don't know how to ask for it without feeling like I'm asking for too much.

I'm not writing this to blame you or make you feel guilty. I'm writing because I don't want my feelings to come out as frustration when what I'm really trying to say is that I want to feel close to you. I want us to understand each other instead of getting stuck in the same misunderstanding.

I know neither of us can be available every second. I know life gets busy and people get tired. I don't expect you to read my mind or always know the perfect thing to say. What I hope for is honesty, communication, and effort that feels genuine from both of us.

When you tell me something and follow through, even in a small way, it helps me trust your words. When you notice that I'm feeling low and ask what's wrong, it makes me feel seen. When we disagree but still try to listen, it reminds me that we're on the same team.

I want to give you that same care, too. Your feelings matter. Your boundaries matter. If something is difficult for you, I want you to feel safe telling me instead of promising something you can't manage. I'd rather we make an honest plan together than have either of us feeling disappointed later.

More than anything, I hope we can make our relationship a place where love is something we show in the everyday moments. Not only through big words, but through patience, kindness, remembering what matters to each other, and being willing to learn.

I'm still learning, too. I won't always get everything right, and I want to be able to hear you when you tell me that. I hope we can both take responsibility for our part, without turning every mistake into a fight about who cares more.

So this is my little letter: I care about you, I care about us, and I want us to keep building something that feels warm and honest for both of us. You don't have to give me a perfect answer after reading this. I just hope you understand my heart a little better.

With love, always ♡

05 · what matters

Reassurance can
be a little thing

Sometimes reassurance isn't a long paragraph or a perfect answer. Sometimes it's a simple message that says, "I'm here, and we'll talk about this."

Sometimes it's letting the other person know when you're busy instead of leaving them guessing. Sometimes it's listening without immediately getting defensive. Sometimes it's remembering a conversation and checking in later.

I want us to be able to ask for comfort without feeling embarrassed. I also want us to be honest when we need space or when we don't know what to say. Both people deserve understanding.

We won't always be able to fix a feeling instantly, and that's okay. What matters to me is knowing that we can talk about it with kindness instead of dismissing it or pretending it never happened.

09 · for the difficult days

When things feel
a little heavy

My love, there will be days when we're tired, when we misunderstand each other, or when neither of us knows exactly what to say. I hope those days don't make us forget the importance of being kind.

We don't have to solve everything in one conversation. It's okay to take a breath, calm down, and return to a difficult topic when we're ready to listen properly.

I hope we can learn to say, "I hear you," even when we see things differently. I hope we can apologize without treating an apology as the whole solution. And I hope we can make changes that are realistic, mutual, and lasting.

Most of all, I hope neither of us feels like we have to earn basic kindness. We both deserve respect, honesty, and the freedom to say what we need.

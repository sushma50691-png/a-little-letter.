My love, I made this because sometimes it's hard to put everything I feel into words when we're talking. I wanted a quiet little place where you could read my heart without either of us feeling rushed.

I love you, and the little things in our relationship matter to me more than I always know how to explain. A message, a little reassurance, remembering something you promised, or making time for us can mean so much.

♡ · ♡ · ♡

It's the little things

I don't expect everything to be perfect. I know we both have busy days, difficult moments, and things to learn. I just want to feel that we're both trying, even in the small ways.

When something is promised and then keeps not happening, I end up feeling disappointed. When I need reassurance, I don't always need a big speech. Sometimes I just need a little patience, honesty, and effort.

Words and actions

Apologies matter to me, and I appreciate them. But what helps me trust again is seeing those words turn into small, consistent actions. I don't want us to keep having the same conversation and feeling hurt afterward.

I'm telling you this because our relationship matters to me. I'm not asking you to read my mind or be perfect. I want us to listen to each other, be honest about what we can do, and make an effort we can both feel.

♡ · ♡ · ♡

What I hope for us

I hope we can make each other feel safe to talk, ask for reassurance without shame, and follow through on the things we say. I want love to feel like care in everyday life, not just something we say when we're scared of losing each other.

I love you. I hope you can understand where I'm coming from, just as I want to understand you. Let's give each other room to speak and let our actions show what we mean.

With love, always ♡
